ForgePulse

Triage the pull request. Measure the blast radius. Keep the policy off GitHub.

ForgePulse sits next to GitHub and scores incoming PRs, records CI failover routing, walks downstream dependents, indexes symbols and owners, and evaluates portable policy from YAML. Your workspace is stored in a durable database and survives restarts.
